Pest Control for Seattle-Area Homes and Businesses

Ants in the kitchen? Mice in the garage?
Call to ask about pest control options for your home or business.

  • Household insects. Describe what you’ve seen and where. If you’re unsure which pest it is, tell the provider what it looks like.
  • Rats and mice. Mention signs such as droppings, gnaw marks or scratching sounds and which rooms are affected.
  • Mosquitoes, fleas and ticks. Let the provider know whether you’ve noticed the problem indoors, outdoors or both.

Residential pest control

When you speak with a provider, ask how they identify the pest, what treatment they suggest and whether follow-up visits are needed. If children or pets use the area, ask how to prepare for the visit.

Can I request service in my area?

Availability depends on your ZIP code and the type of pest service you need. When you call, tell us where the property is and what you’ve noticed.
